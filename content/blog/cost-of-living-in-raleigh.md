## What the Cost of Living in Raleigh Looks Like in 2026

Housing is the biggest line for most households. USAFacts, using Census Bureau survey data, puts the [Raleigh metro's median rent at about $1,573 a month for 2020 to 2024](https://usafacts.org/answers/how-much-do-households-spend-on-rent/metro-area/raleigh-nc/). That is a metro-wide median: a newer one-bedroom near North Hills or downtown usually costs more, and an older unit farther out costs less.

Pay is the other side of the ledger. The Bureau of Labor Statistics reports that [workers in the Raleigh-Cary area averaged $34.01 an hour in May 2025](https://www.bls.gov/regions/southeast/news-release/2026/occupationalemploymentandwages_raleigh_20260708.htm), against a national average of $33.54. Before you sign a lease, check your own offer against the rent you are looking at, and add a car payment if you are coming from a city where you didn't need one. Raleigh is built around driving.

## What Raleigh Utilities Cost Each Month

Water and sewer come from the city; electricity comes from a separate utility, and the two behave very differently.

The [City of Raleigh's water rates](https://raleighnc.gov/doing-business/services/utility-rates-deposits-other-charges), effective July 1, 2026, charge residential customers inside city limits by the CCF, which is 748 gallons:

- Tier 1, up to 4 CCF a month: $3.11 per CCF.
- Tier 2, 5 to 20 CCF: $5.41 per CCF.
- Tier 3, 21 CCF and up: $6.97 per CCF.
- A watershed protection fee of $0.1122 per CCF on all usage.

A two-person apartment using 4 CCF a month stays in Tier 1, so the water itself comes to under $13. Fixed charges and sewer are billed on the same statement, so the total is higher.

Electricity is the line that swings. Raleigh summers are long, hot and humid, and air conditioning drives the bill from late spring into early fall. Ask the landlord or seller for the last twelve months of power bills; that history is more useful than any estimate.

## North Carolina Taxes That Shape a Raleigh Budget

North Carolina's personal income tax is a flat rate, and [the rate for 2026 is 3.99%](https://www.carolinajournal.com/federal-state-tax-changes-to-hit-nc-taxpayers-in-2026/), down from 4.25% in 2025. For someone leaving a state with a higher or graduated rate, the difference shows up in the first paycheck.

Property tax is where costs are rising. Wake County's [2026 property tax bills are calculated at 53.71 cents per $100 of assessed value](https://www.wake.gov/departments-government/tax-administration/tax-bill-help/2026-property-tax-bills), after a two-cent increase, and balances paid by January 5, 2027 avoid interest. On a home assessed at $400,000, the county portion works out to about $2,148 a year. The City of Raleigh and any special district add their own rates to the same bill, so look up the tax history for the specific parcel rather than estimating from the county rate alone.
