## Plan Truck Parking Around Arlington's Overnight Rule

Arlington has a parking rule that catches people who rent a truck the night before. The town's [overnight parking page](https://www.arlingtonma.gov/departments/police/traffic-parking/overnight-parking) says parking on any public street for more than an hour between 1 a.m. and 7 a.m. is prohibited unless you have a waiver or permit. Residents can request an overnight waiver, which covers one vehicle for one night in front of the property, before 10 p.m. on the night it's needed, and each property gets fourteen a year.

If you're hiring a crew, ask how the truck will reach your door and where it will stand while loading. If you're renting a truck yourself, either return it the same day or sort out a waiver in advance. Snow emergencies override waivers, so a winter move needs a backup plan for parking.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a Moving Truck Park Overnight on an Arlington Street?

Not without permission. Arlington prohibits parking on any public street for more than an hour between 1 a.m. and 7 a.m. unless you have a waiver or permit. Residents can request a one-night waiver for one vehicle in front of their property before 10 p.m., and snow emergencies override waivers entirely.

### How Much Notice Do Arlington Movers Need?

Book four to six weeks ahead for most Arlington moves, and earlier between May and September or for a weekend date. Fixed closing dates and lease ends leave little room to shift, so lock in the crew first. Booking early also gives you time to set up storage if your move-out and move-in dates don't match.
