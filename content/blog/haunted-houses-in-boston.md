Most haunted houses in Boston aren't actually in Boston. The big scare attractions sit 30 to 70 minutes out, in towns like Abington, Danvers and Westford or over the New Hampshire line, while the city itself trades on ghost tours and burying grounds older than the country. If you moved here on September 1, that split matters, because half of these trips need a car and the other half need the T. This guide covers the haunted houses worth the drive, the haunted places you can walk to, and how Salem handled Halloween in 2026, so you can plan next October early.

## Key Takeaways

- The closest major haunted house to the city is Barrett's Haunted Mansion in Abington, about 30 minutes by car, with 2026 tickets from $20.
- Witch's Woods in Westford and Spooky World in Litchfield, NH sat at the top of the 2026 price range, at $52 and from $57.
- Boston's haunted history is walkable: the Granary Burying Ground on Tremont Street is open daily from about 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.
- Salem's Haunted Happenings runs all of October and draws more than a million visitors, so take the Commuter Rail from North Station.
- Moving to or from Salem in October? Pick a weekday, because parking there is limited all month.

Haunted houses around the city are seasonal, ticketed and mostly suburban. [Meet Boston's guide to haunted experiences](https://www.meetboston.com/blog/post/best-haunted-houses-in-boston/), from the city's convention and visitors bureau, listed these 2026 starting prices and drive times:

Those drive times assume light traffic, and late-October weekends rarely deliver it. Before you commit:

- Check the attraction's own calendar, since most open only on select nights.
- Treat the prices as a floor, because they're starting prices and change each season.
- Plan on a car or a rideshare, since most of these are hard to reach by transit.

## Haunted Places in Boston You Can Reach on Foot

Boston's own ghost stories are downtown. The city looks after sixteen historic burying grounds dating from 1630 to 1841, and three of them, Granary, King's Chapel and Copp's Hill, sit on the Freedom Trail. According to the [city's Historic Burying Grounds Initiative](https://www.boston.gov/departments/parks-and-recreation/historic-burying-grounds-initiative), the Granary Burying Ground at 120 Tremont Street is open daily from about 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. See it in daylight, then come back for the after-dark version with a guide.

Ghost tours are the easiest way into that side of the city after sunset. Starting prices on the same Meet Boston list:

- Freedom Trail lantern tour: $17
- Haunted Boston walking ghost tour: $21
- Ghosts and Gravestones trolley tour: from $30.95
- Ghost tour of the Charles River, leaving from Cambridge: $40

Most of these start downtown, so the subway gets you there without a car. If you live across the river, [moving in Cambridge](/cambridge/movers/) puts the river cruise a short walk away.

## How Salem Celebrates Halloween

The biggest Halloween event in the region is a short train ride north. [Destination Salem's Haunted Happenings announcement](https://www.salem.org/blog/salem-haunted-happenings-returns-this-october/) describes a month of events that draws more than a million visitors, and in 2026 it doubled as the city's 400th anniversary. On Halloween night, which fell on a Saturday, live music ran downtown from noon and a 400-drone show flew over the North River at 10 p.m.

Don't drive. The [MBTA's Salem travel page](https://www.mbta.com/destinations/salem) says trains leave North Station, a one-way ticket costs $8 and the ride takes about 30 minutes, and a $10 weekend pass covers unlimited Commuter Rail trips on Saturdays and Sundays. It also warns that parking in Salem is limited in October, and on Halloween 2026 no vehicles could enter or leave the Salem station garage between 6 p.m. and 11:30 p.m.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is the Best Haunted House Near Boston?

It depends on how far you'll drive and how scared you want to be. Barrett's Haunted Mansion in Abington is about 30 minutes from the city, with 2026 tickets from $20. For a bigger night out, Witch's Woods in Westford is about 45 minutes away, with tickets from $52 according to Meet Boston's list.

### Can You Visit Haunted Places in Boston Without a Car?

Yes. Boston's haunted history is concentrated downtown, so the subway and a walking tour cover it. The Granary Burying Ground on Tremont Street is open daily from about 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., and evening tours start at $17. Salem works without a car too, since the Commuter Rail from North Station takes about 30 minutes.

### How Much Do Haunted Houses Near Boston Cost?

In 2026, haunted houses near Boston started at about $20 per ticket and ran past $55. Barrett's Haunted Mansion started around $20, Factory of Terror around $27, Fright Kingdom and Haunted Overload at $38, Witch's Woods at $52, and Spooky World from $57, based on Meet Boston's list for the season.

### Is October a Good Month to Move to Salem?

October is a hard month to move in Salem. Haunted Happenings runs all month and brings more than a million visitors, and the MBTA warns that parking in the city is limited in October. A weekday move earlier in the month is far easier than a Saturday, and November is easier still if your dates are flexible.
