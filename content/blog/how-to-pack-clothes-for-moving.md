Here is how to pack clothes for moving without unpacking a pile of wrinkled laundry at the other end: leave hanging garments on their hangers in a wardrobe box or a trash bag bundle, and fold or roll everything else into medium boxes you can lift comfortably. Which mix suits you depends on your stairs, your move date and which pieces you couldn't replace. Below are both hanging methods, a fold-or-roll guide for boxed clothes, the box weight worth sticking to on a walk-up, what to carry yourself, and how to label boxes so the crew puts them in the closet they came from.

## Key Takeaways

- Hanging clothes can travel in a wardrobe box or in a heavy-duty trash bag pulled over 10 to 15 light garments on their hangers.
- Fold structured pieces such as jeans and sweaters flat, and roll soft items such as T-shirts and pajamas.
- Keep clothing boxes to about 40 pounds. The NIOSH lifting equation starts at 51 pounds for an ideal lift, and a stairwell isn't ideal.
- Carry jewelry, furs and anything irreplaceable yourself, since FMCSA notes that damage to boxes you pack can be harder to claim.
- On an hourly local move, clothes that are sealed and labeled by room before the crew arrives save time you would pay for.

## How to Pack Clothes for Moving: Sort Before You Box

Start about three weeks out with clothes you won't wear before the move, like winter coats in spring. Pack everyday clothes during the last week, and keep about seven days of outfits in a bag that rides with you.

Sorting first saves boxes, and on an hourly move every box is time. Donate what you haven't worn in a year, then split the rest into three piles:

- Hang: coats, dresses, suits and anything that creases badly
- Fold: jeans, sweaters, T-shirts, pajamas and gym clothes
- Carry yourself: jewelry, furs, a wedding dress and anything you can't replace

Your destination can change the sort. The [National Weather Service's December normals for Charlotte](https://www.weather.gov/media/gsp/Climate/CLT/CLTDECNORM.pdf) show an average high of 54.9°F and a low of 34.5°F, so a parka built for a Boston January gets far fewer wearable days if you're [moving to Charlotte](/charlotte/movers/). That's a good coat to sell or donate before it takes up truck space.

## How to Pack Hanging Clothes for Moving

You have two practical options for hanging clothes. A wardrobe box costs more and protects better. A trash bag bundle costs almost nothing and works for everyday pieces on a short move.

### Moving Hanging Clothes in a Wardrobe Box

A wardrobe box is a tall carton with a metal bar, so you lift clothes off the closet rod and hang them inside without folding. Use it for suits, dresses and long coats, and for a [long-distance move](/long-distance-movers/), where boxes get stacked and strapped for hours. Push the hangers tight together so they can't slide, and fill the floor of the box with shoes or folded sweaters so it doesn't tip.

### The Trash Bag Method for Hanging Clothes

Use contractor-grade bags, because thin kitchen bags tear at the hooks.

1. Group 10 to 15 light garments on their hangers, or fewer for heavy coats.
2. Pull a bag up over the garments from the bottom.
3. Poke the hooks through a small hole in the closed end of the bag.
4. Gather the open end under the hems and tie it shut.
5. Write the room or the owner's name on a strip of tape.

A bundle is fine for a few days. If clothes will sit in storage for weeks or months, switch to breathable garment bags so moisture doesn't get trapped.

## Packing Clothes for Moving in Boxes

Fold structured clothing and roll soft clothing. It keeps boxes compact and avoids hard creases.

| Method | Best For | Watch Out For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat fold | Jeans, sweaters, dress pants, hoodies | Put the heaviest pieces at the bottom |
| Roll | T-shirts, gym clothes, pajamas | Skip it for pieces that hold a shape |
| Wardrobe box | Suits, dresses, long coats | Costs more than a trash bag bundle |
| Trash bag bundle | Everyday hanging clothes | A short-term wrapper, not storage |
| Suitcase or duffel | Your first week of outfits | Keep it with you, off the truck |

Use medium boxes rather than large ones. Clothes feel light, but a large box of jeans adds up to a heavy lift. [NIOSH researchers](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/188743/cdc_188743_DS1.pdf) start the revised lifting equation at 51 pounds for an ideal lift and lower the limit as the task gets harder. Carrying a box down a fourth-floor walk-up is far from ideal, so 40 pounds or less per box is a sensible target.

## What to Carry Yourself and How to Label the Rest

Keep jewelry, furs, a wedding dress and anything you can't replace in your own car. [FMCSA's guidance on liability](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/consumer-protection/protect-your-move/are-you-moving/liability-protection) warns that if you pack a box yourself and its contents are damaged, the claim may be harder to establish, and released value on an interstate move pays no more than 60 cents per pound per article. A 3-pound wool coat would come to $1.80 under that level.

Label every box on two sides with the room and a short note, such as "primary closet, coats." ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is It Better to Pack Clothes in Boxes or Bags When Moving?

Use boxes for folded clothes and wardrobe boxes or trash bag bundles for hanging clothes. Boxes stack, keep their shape and carry labels, while loose bags shift in the truck and can trap moisture if clothes sit for weeks. For most local moves, sealed and labeled boxes are the safer default for anything folded.

### How Long Before a Move Should I Start Packing Clothes?

Start about three weeks before the move with off-season clothes, then pack everyday clothes in the final week. Keep around seven days of outfits, pajamas and work clothes out until move day. If the move is less than two weeks away, sort into hang, fold and carry piles first, then pack each closet in one session.

### Can Movers Move a Dresser With Clothes Still Inside?

Often, if the dresser is sturdy and the drawers hold only light clothing, but confirm it with your mover first. Take out books, liquids, glass and anything heavy, because a full dresser weighs far more than an empty one. Mention loaded dressers when you ask for a quote, so the crew plans for the weight.

### What Is the Best Way to Pack Clothes for a Long-Distance Move?

Use sealed, labeled boxes for folded clothes and wardrobe boxes for hanging pieces, because loose bags shift over a long drive. Keep valuable items with you, and choose your coverage level before the truck leaves. Tell the mover roughly how many boxes and wardrobe boxes of clothes to expect so the quote matches the load.
