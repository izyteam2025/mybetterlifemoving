These local moving tips for families are written for the Boston version of the job: a triple-decker with a steep back stair, a street with no legal place for a truck, a school calendar that leaves one free week, and a toddler who wants to help carry the lamp. Moving across town with kids is still a full move. Every bed, crib and box gets lifted, loaded and carried up somewhere else. The difference is that a family has less slack for a bad day. Below is how to time it, budget it, prepare the street and the house, and choose a crew you are comfortable having in your home.

## Key Takeaways

- Avoid the days around September 1 if your lease allows it, because trucks, crews and street space are scarce that week.
- Book a city moving truck permit in Boston at least 15 days ahead so the crew isn't carrying furniture half a block.
- Local moves are billed hourly, so packing and decluttering before the crew arrives lowers the bill.
- Massachusetts requires household movers to hold a Department of Public Utilities license, and you can check it before booking.
- Keep children and pets away from doorways and stairs while the crew loads.

## What Makes a Boston Move Harder With Kids

The city itself sets most of the obstacles. Narrow one-way streets in Beacon Hill, the North End and parts of Somerville leave little room to park a truck. Storrow Drive's low overpasses are off-limits to moving trucks, and every year someone in a rental truck learns that the hard way. Older buildings add tight turns, walk-up stairs and doorways that were not built for a modern sectional.

With kids in the house, those obstacles cost more. A blocked street means a longer carry, and a longer carry means more time with the front door open and small children underfoot. That is the case for planning the street and the building as carefully as the boxes.

## Timing a Family Move Around School and September 1

Most Boston leases turn over at the start of September, which is also when school starts. Families who can move in July, or in the first weeks of August, usually find more crews available and more space at the curb.

- Move during a school break if you can, so children aren't changing routines mid-term.
- If you must move near September 1, book the crew as soon as the new lease is signed.
- Apply for the city's [moving truck permit](https://www.boston.gov/departments/parking-clerk/reserve-parking-spot-your-moving-truck), which reserves two spaces from 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. Online applications need at least 15 days' notice.
- Plan where the kids will be on moving day: with a relative, at a friend's house, or at the new place with one parent while the other stays with the crew.

If the old lease ends before the new one starts, [storage between your two homes](/moving-and-storage/) keeps the furniture out of a relative's garage.

## Choosing Movers You'll Let Into Your Home

Movers spend hours inside your home, around your children, pets and keepsakes. Check them before you book.

- Licensing. Massachusetts requires that [household moving companies be licensed by the Department of Public Utilities](https://www.mass.gov/guides/moving-within-massachusetts). Ask for the certificate number and look it up.
- A real company. The FMCSA's moving fraud brochure lists warning signs such as [a phone answered only with "movers" or "moving company"](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/sites/fmcsa.dot.gov/files/docs/Protect_Your_Move_Brochure.pdf) instead of a business name, and a rental truck arriving on moving day.
- A written quote. Get it after the mover has seen what you are moving, in person or by video.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is the Best Time for a Family to Move in Boston?

The best time for a family to move in Boston is usually July or early August, before the September 1 lease rush and before school starts. Crews and street space are easier to book, and children get a few weeks to settle in. Mid-month weekdays are the easiest dates of all if your work schedule allows.

### How Do I Keep Kids Safe on Moving Day?

Keep young children and pets away from doors, stairs and the truck while the crew is loading. Arrange childcare or a relative's house for the busiest hours, or give older kids a job in a room that is already empty. Pack a bag with snacks, chargers, medicine and a favorite toy that stays with you.

### Do I Need a Parking Permit to Move in Boston?

A permit isn't required everywhere, but in dense neighborhoods it is often the only way to get the truck near your door. The City of Boston permit reserves two spaces for the day, from 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. Online applications must be submitted at least 15 days before your move date.

### How Can a Family Lower the Cost of a Local Move?

Lower the cost of a local move by cutting the hours. Declutter before packing, have every box sealed and labelled before the crew arrives, strip the beds, empty dressers that need to be carried down stairs, and reserve parking and elevators at both addresses. With hourly billing, every minute of waiting shows up on the bill.
