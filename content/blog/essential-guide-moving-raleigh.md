## Why Boston Residents Are Moving to Raleigh

Most people we move south name the same three reasons: housing, work and winter. Raleigh sits in the Research Triangle with Durham and Chapel Hill, close to NC State, Duke and UNC, and the region's tech, research and healthcare employers keep hiring. Pay holds up better than many expect. The Bureau of Labor Statistics found that [Raleigh-Cary workers averaged $34.01 an hour in May 2025](https://www.bls.gov/regions/southeast/news-release/2026/occupationalemploymentandwages_raleigh_20260708.htm), slightly above the national mean of $33.54.

A few things change the day you arrive:

- Raleigh is built around the car. If you were car-free in Boston, budget for a vehicle before you sign a lease.
- Summers are long, hot and humid, so air conditioning matters more than a radiator ever did.
- Space goes further. Many people coming from a Boston one-bedroom end up with rooms to furnish, which is worth knowing before you sell the couch.

## How a Boston to Raleigh Move Is Priced and Regulated

An interstate move works differently from a local Boston job. Local moves are billed by the hour. A long-haul move is priced on what goes on the truck and how far it travels, and federal rules apply from pickup to delivery.

### Check the Carrier's Federal Registration

Any company hauling household goods across state lines has to be registered with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration. Ask for the USDOT and MC numbers and look them up yourself before you pay anything. Ours are USDOT 3866930, MC 1414489. We are a carrier, so the crew that loads in Brighton or Back Bay works for us and drives our truck.

### Get the Quote From a Real Inventory

A long distance quote is only as good as the list behind it. Walk the mover through every room, closet and storage cage, in person or on a video call, and point out anything heavy or fragile. The FMCSA handbook *Your Rights and Responsibilities When You Move* explains that on a non-binding quote [the mover cannot require you to pay more than 110 percent at delivery](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/sites/fmcsa.dot.gov/files/2023-10/FMCSA_R%26R_Handbook_Web_v1.pdf), with any balance billed later. Interstate movers must give you that booklet, so read it before moving day.

### What Moves the Price Up or Down

- Volume: a fully furnished two-bedroom weighs far more than a sparse one.
- Season: May through September is the busy stretch for long-haul moves, and dates go first.

## Timing Pickup, Delivery and Storage Between Leases

Your belongings won't arrive the same evening you do. Ask for the delivery window in writing and plan your own travel, a hotel night or a stay with friends around it. Someone has to be at the Raleigh address to check items against the inventory and sign.

Lease dates rarely line up. A Boston lease ends on the 31st, the Raleigh closing slips to the 10th, and suddenly a household needs a home for ten days. Book storage with the move rather than scrambling for it later. With us, storage is priced by the cubic feet your items take up, and the same company picks up, holds and delivers, so you deal with one contract. On the Raleigh end, ask your building or HOA about loading dock reservations, gate codes and parking for a large truck before the crew leaves Boston. A new-construction community in Wake Forest or Holly Springs may have rules a Cambridge walk-up never did.

## Settling In: Raleigh Paperwork and Daily Life

The paperwork after the move is short but has deadlines. North Carolina's DMV gives new residents [60 days from establishing residence to get a state license and register their vehicles](https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/help/moving/Pages/new-residents.aspx), and registration can be due sooner if your old state's grace period runs out first.

- File a mail forward before you leave Boston. The Postal Service says [standard mail forwarding lasts 12 months](https://www.usps.com/manage/forward.htm?kbid=93121), with paid extensions available.
- Update your bank, insurer, employer and voter registration with the new address.
- Transfer medical records before you go, especially for children.
