Knowing how to move a refrigerator comes down to three habits: prep it a day ahead, keep it upright whenever you can, and protect the floor on the way out. Most fridge damage on moving day happens before it reaches the truck. A compressor gets switched on before its oil settles, an ice maker line gets yanked loose, or a hardwood floor gets gouged by a fridge walked out of its alcove. Below are the prep steps, what GE, LG and Whirlpool say about laying a fridge down, how to get it out without marking the floor, and what to do with the washer and dryer on the same day.

## Key Takeaways

- Empty, unplug and defrost the refrigerator at least 24 hours before the move, and disconnect the water line if it has an ice maker.
- Keep it upright. GE says French-door and bottom-freezer models must stay upright, though they can be tilted through doorways.
- If it traveled on its side, stand it up before plugging it in. Manufacturer wait times range from 15 minutes to 24 hours.
- Cover the floor with cardboard or hardboard and load the appliance dolly from the side, never the front or back.
- A front-load washer needs new transport bolts before it moves, according to Maytag, and has to travel upright.

## How to Move a Refrigerator: What to Do the Day Before

You can do all of the prep yourself, even if a crew does the lifting. Start the day before, because a fridge that's still dripping leaks all over the truck.

- Eat down the food and put the rest in a cooler.
- Unplug it, prop the doors open to defrost, and wrap the glass shelves and drawers separately.
- If it has an ice maker or water dispenser, turn off the supply valve, disconnect the line and empty the ice bin.
- Tape the doors shut with painter's tape so the finish isn't marked, and tape the power cord to the back.
- Measure the fridge, then every doorway, hallway and stair turn between the kitchen and the curb.

If the fridge is part of a whole kitchen, box the pantry the same week. [Kitchen packing](/packing-services/) can be added to a quote if you'd rather not do it yourself.

## Can You Lay a Refrigerator Down to Move It?

Sometimes, but upright is always the safer choice. Laying a fridge down can let compressor oil move out of place, and running it before the oil settles back can damage the compressor. The manufacturers don't fully agree, so check your brand:

| --- | --- | --- |
| GE | [GE's moving instructions](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=16603) say French-door and bottom-freezer models must stay upright. Side-by-sides go on the freezer side, top-freezers on the side opposite the hinges. | Stand it upright as long as it lay down. After more than a day on its side, wait 24 hours. |
| LG | [LG's guidance on moving a fridge on its side](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/can-i-move-my-lg-refrigerator-on-its-side-how-to-move-and-install-it-safely-CT00023125-20155255782623) says to always keep it upright. | At least 2 hours if it had to go on its side through a doorway. |
| Whirlpool | [Whirlpool's moving care page](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Refrigeration/Full-Size_Refrigerators/French_Door_Bottom_Freezer_Refrigerator/Other_Concerns/Proper_Moving_Care_-_Refrigerator) says it can travel upright or lying down. | 15 minutes for R-134a units, 24 hours for older R-12 units. |

The rule of thumb: if it can go upright, it goes upright. A fridge standing against the truck wall is also the easiest thing on the load to strap tight.

## How to Transport a Refrigerator Without Scratching the Floor

The costly damage is usually the floor, not the fridge. In a rental, a gouge across the kitchen floor can come out of your security deposit, and narrow stairwells in older buildings leave little room to turn.

1. Cover the path from the fridge to the door with cardboard or hardboard, as Whirlpool recommends, and tape the seams so dolly wheels don't catch.
2. Raise the front leveling legs and pull the fridge out in a line instead of walking it side to side.
3. Wrap it in moving blankets and stretch wrap to protect the finish and keep the doors closed.
4. Slide the appliance dolly in from the side and strap the fridge on before you tilt. GE warns that loading from the front or back can cause damage.
5. Take stairs one step at a time, with one person below taking the weight and one above steering.
6. In the truck, stand it upright against the wall and strap it so it can't shift.

## How to Move a Washer and Dryer With the Fridge

If the fridge is going, the laundry pair usually is too, and the washer has one step people miss.

- Turn off both water valves, disconnect the hoses and drain them into a bucket.
- Tuck the hoses inside the drum and tie up the power cord.
- For front-load washers, [Maytag's moving instructions](https://producthelp.maytag.com/Laundry/Washers/Front_Load_Washers/Wash_Performance_or_Clothing_Results/Cleaning_and_Maintenance/Moving_a_Front_Load_Washer) say to call for service to install new transport bolts, never reuse old ones, and move the washer upright.
- For the dryer, empty the lint trap, disconnect the vent and tape the door shut.
- If the dryer runs on gas, have a licensed gas fitter shut off and cap the line.

Book the transport bolt visit a week ahead. Move morning is too late to schedule it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Long Should a Refrigerator Sit Before You Plug It In After Moving?

Check your brand's rule first. LG asks for at least two hours after time on its side, Whirlpool asks for 15 minutes upright on R-134a models, and GE says to match the time it lay down, up to 24 hours after a day or more. If you don't know the rule, waiting a few hours costs nothing.

### Can You Transport a Refrigerator in a Pickup Truck?

A pickup can work if the fridge stands upright, strapped against the front of the bed and padded with blankets. A full-size refrigerator won't fit upright in a car, so a car only works for a compact or dorm fridge. Laying a French-door or bottom-freezer model flat in a truck bed goes against GE's guidance.

### Are Most Refrigerators on Wheels?

Many full-size refrigerators have small rollers at the base, but they're made for sliding the fridge in and out of its space, not for rolling it across a room. Raise the front leveling legs, pull it out onto cardboard, and switch to an appliance dolly once it clears the cabinets.
