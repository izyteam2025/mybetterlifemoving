Choosing between commercial moving companies in Boston is less about finding a big name and more about finding a crew that can move your office without costing you a workday. A business move has a budget, a lease deadline, staff who need their desks on Monday, and equipment nobody wants to replace. This guide covers what to check before you hire, how to plan an office move around your operations, how commercial quotes are put together, and the permits and paperwork that catch businesses out. It is written from the moving side, by the crews at Better Life Moving in Brighton, Boston.

## Key Takeaways

- In Massachusetts, household moving companies must hold a DPU license, so ask any mover for its certificate number and check it.
- Ask who will do the work: a carrier with its own crews and trucks, or a broker passing your job to another company.
- Plan the move around your business hours, with IT, furniture and files on separate labelled schedules.
- A Boston street move often needs a city truck permit, applied for online at least 15 days ahead.
- Get coverage terms in writing before moving day, especially for computers and specialty equipment.

## What to Look for in a Boston Commercial Mover

Start with who the company is and who will actually carry your furniture. A commercial move puts a crew inside your office, often after hours, with access to equipment and files, so the basics matter.

- Registration and licensing. Massachusetts says [household moving companies must be licensed by the Department of Public Utilities](https://www.mass.gov/guides/moving-within-massachusetts). For an office leaving the state, the company also needs FMCSA registration with a USDOT number. Ours is USDOT 3866930, MC 1414489.
- Own crews or a broker. A carrier sends its own people and trucks. A broker sells the job on, and you may never meet the crew in advance.
- Experience with your type of space. A two-room accounting office is a different job from a retail floor with fixtures and stock.
- Coverage in writing. Ask what happens if a monitor or a server rack is damaged, and get the answer on paper.

## Planning an Office Move Without Losing a Workday

The best office moves look boring from the inside. Staff pack their desks on Friday afternoon, the crew works the evening or weekend, and Monday starts at the new address with phones and internet running.

### Build the Timeline Backward

1. Set the target day and check it against the old lease end, the new lease start and any building move-in windows.
2. Book the mover and confirm loading dock and elevator reservations at both buildings.
3. Order the new internet service early, because installation dates are the slowest item on most office lists.
4. Assign one person inside the company to answer the crew's questions on the day.

### Label by Room and Desk, Not by Contents

Number every desk and office on a floor plan of the new space, and put the same number on every box and chair. The crew can then place each item without asking where it goes.

### Handle the Paperwork

Boston street parking for the truck is usually the first permit question. The city's [moving truck permit](https://www.boston.gov/departments/parking-clerk/reserve-parking-spot-your-moving-truck) reserves two spaces from 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. and needs an online application at least 15 days before the move. After the move, update your business address with clients, banks and agencies. The IRS accepts [Form 8822-B for a business change of address](https://irs.gov/addresschange) or a signed written statement.

## How Commercial Moving Quotes Work

A quote should show what you are paying for, line by line. For moves within the Boston area:

- Local moves are billed hourly, with travel time from our yard added.
- There is a 2-hour minimum that includes travel time.
- Blankets, shrink wrap, tape and truck costs (gas, mileage, tolls) are included in the hourly rate.

The hours depend on the volume of furniture, how far the truck is from the door, elevator access and how much is packed before the crew arrives. Long distance moves are quoted at a flat rate, built from an inventory of what is moving.

If the new space isn't ready on the day the old lease ends, storage is priced by the cubic feet your items take up. Ask about [business storage between leases](/moving-and-storage/) when you request the quote. Coverage belongs in the quote conversation too. For interstate shipments, the FMCSA notes that basic released value protection [makes the mover responsible for no more than 60 cents per pound per article](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/protect-your-move/valuation-insurance), which is very little for a laptop or a monitor. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Should I Look for in a Commercial Moving Company in Boston?

Look for a commercial mover that holds a Massachusetts DPU license for local work and FMCSA registration for any move across state lines, runs its own crews and trucks, puts its quote and coverage terms in writing, and can schedule around your business hours. Ask how many moves of your size and type the company handles.

### How Much Does a Commercial Move in Boston Cost?

The cost depends on the volume of furniture and equipment, access at both buildings, distance and how much is packed in advance. Better Life Moving bills local moves hourly, with travel time from our yard added and a 2-hour minimum that includes travel time. Long distance moves are quoted at a flat rate based on an inventory.

### Can an Office Move Happen After Hours or on a Weekend?

Yes, many office moves are scheduled for evenings or weekends so staff lose as little working time as possible. Check your building's rules first, because some properties limit when the loading dock and freight elevator can be used. Book those reservations at both addresses as soon as the move date is set.

### Do Commercial Movers Pack Office Equipment?

Many do, and it is worth discussing at the quote stage. Staff can usually pack their own desks, while the crew handles furniture, wrapping and loading. Fragile or high-value items such as artwork and display pieces often get separate packing. Back up all computer data before the move, whoever packs the hardware.

### How Far Ahead Should a Business Book Movers?

Book four to eight weeks ahead for most office moves, and earlier if your date falls at the end of a month or near September 1, when Boston's residential moves take up crews and street space. Ordering internet service and reserving building elevators can take longer than booking the mover, so start those at the same time.
